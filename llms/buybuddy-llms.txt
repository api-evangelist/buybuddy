# BuyBuddy

> BuyBuddy is an AI-powered retail merchandising platform ("AI Merchandiser that tracks, thinks & acts for you") that automates in-store operations for retailers and brands — planogram compliance and shelf monitoring via computer vision, stockout prevention, product placement optimization, dynamic pricing and promotions, demand forecasting, store transfers, and shopper-behavior analysis through smart tags. BuyBuddy is a portfolio company of 500 Global. As of this profile no public developer API, documentation, or specification surface has been published; the entry is maintained as a company profile in the API Evangelist network.
